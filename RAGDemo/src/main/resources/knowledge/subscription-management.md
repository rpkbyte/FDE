# NovaTech Solutions — Subscription Management

## 1. Subscription Overview

A subscription provides access to the features and services included in the customer's selected plan.

Subscription features, limits, billing frequency, and cancellation rules depend on the selected product and plan.

## 2. Starting a Subscription

Customers can subscribe to an available paid plan through the supported purchase or checkout process.

Before completing a subscription, customers should review:

* Selected plan
* Available features
* Usage limits
* Billing frequency
* Price
* Applicable taxes
* Cancellation terms
* Refund terms

## 3. Subscription Status

A subscription may have different statuses depending on the billing system.

Possible statuses include:

* Active
* Trial
* Past due
* Suspended
* Cancelled
* Expired

The exact statuses supported depend on the product.

## 4. Active Subscription

An active subscription normally allows the customer to use the features included in their selected plan, subject to applicable usage limits and service terms.

## 5. Subscription Renewal

Recurring subscriptions may automatically renew according to their billing schedule.

Customers who do not want their subscription to renew should cancel it according to the applicable cancellation procedure before the next renewal.

## 6. Subscription Cancellation

Customers may be able to cancel a subscription through account settings or by contacting customer support.

Cancellation generally prevents future renewal according to the applicable subscription terms.

Cancellation does not automatically mean that the customer receives a refund for a previous payment.

## 7. Access After Cancellation

Depending on the product, a cancelled subscription may remain active until the end of the already-paid billing period.

The exact access period depends on the applicable subscription terms.

## 8. Upgrading a Subscription

Customers may be able to upgrade their current plan.

An upgrade can provide:

* Additional features
* Higher usage limits
* Premium functionality
* Additional support capabilities

Any additional charge or billing adjustment depends on the applicable billing system.

## 9. Downgrading a Subscription

Customers may be able to downgrade their subscription.

A downgrade may result in:

* Fewer features
* Lower usage limits
* Removal of premium functionality
* Changes to available support

Customers should review the new plan before confirming a downgrade.

## 10. Changing Billing Frequency

Where supported, customers may change their billing frequency.

Available options may include:

* Monthly
* Annual

The billing adjustment depends on the applicable subscription and billing system.

## 11. Failed Renewal

If an automatic subscription renewal fails, the billing system may retry the payment.

Depending on the applicable terms, the subscription may later become:

* Past due
* Restricted
* Suspended
* Cancelled

Customers should update their payment method if the renewal fails.

## 12. Expired Subscription

An expired subscription may no longer provide access to paid features.

Customers who want to regain access may need to purchase or reactivate an available subscription.

## 13. Reactivating a Subscription

Some products may allow customers to reactivate a cancelled or expired subscription.

The availability of reactivation depends on the product and subscription system.

Customers should follow the reactivation process provided by the product.

## 14. Plan Changes and Existing Data

Changing a subscription plan may affect access to features or usage limits.

Customers should review the applicable plan terms before changing plans.

The effect of a plan change on stored data depends on the product.

## 15. Subscription and Refunds

Subscription cancellation and refunds are separate processes.

Cancelling a subscription does not automatically mean that the latest payment will be refunded.

Refund eligibility is determined according to the applicable Refund and Cancellation Policy.

## 16. Subscription Receipts

Customers may receive a receipt or invoice after a successful subscription payment.

Billing records may be available through the customer's account depending on the product.

## 17. Subscription Problems

Customers experiencing subscription problems should check:

* Current subscription status
* Payment status
* Selected plan
* Billing history
* Account email address

If the subscription status appears incorrect, customers should contact support.

## 18. Enterprise Subscriptions

Enterprise subscriptions may use customized terms.

Enterprise customers may have:

* Custom plans
* Custom billing schedules
* Custom usage limits
* Contract-specific cancellation terms
* Custom support arrangements

Enterprise subscription questions should be handled according to the applicable contract.

## 19. Subscription Support Rules

The AI support assistant must:

* Explain only documented subscription rules.
* Never claim that a subscription is active without account information confirming it.
* Never claim that a subscription has been cancelled without confirmation.
* Never promise a refund.
* Never invent renewal dates.
* Never invent plan features.
* Never expose another customer's subscription information.
* Direct account-specific subscription issues to appropriate support channels.

## 20. Common Subscription Questions

### How do I subscribe to a paid plan?

Choose an available paid plan and complete the supported checkout process.

### How do I cancel my subscription?

Use the cancellation option available in your account settings or contact customer support if self-service cancellation is not available.

### Will I get a refund if I cancel?

Cancellation and refunds are separate. Refund eligibility depends on the applicable refund policy.

### Will I still have access after cancelling?

Depending on the product, access may continue until the end of the already-paid billing period.

### Can I upgrade my plan?

Customers may be able to upgrade depending on the product and available plans.

### Can I downgrade my plan?

Customers may be able to downgrade depending on the product and subscription terms.

### What happens if my renewal payment fails?

The system may retry the payment or restrict, suspend, or cancel the subscription according to the applicable terms.

### Can I reactivate a cancelled subscription?

Some products support subscription reactivation. Availability depends on the product and subscription system.

### How do I know whether my subscription is active?

Check the subscription section of your account if the product provides subscription status information.

### My subscription status looks incorrect. What should I do?

Check your billing history and payment status. If the information still appears incorrect, contact NovaTech Solutions support.
