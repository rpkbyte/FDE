
# NovaTech Solutions — Refund and Cancellation Policy

## 1. Policy Overview

This document describes the general refund and cancellation rules for NovaTech Solutions products and services.

Refund eligibility may vary depending on:

* Product or service
* Subscription plan
* Purchase type
* Payment method
* Applicable agreement
* Applicable law

Customers should review the terms applicable to their specific purchase.

## 2. Subscription Cancellation

Customers may be able to cancel a recurring subscription through their account settings or by contacting customer support.

Cancellation procedures may vary depending on the product.

Customers should confirm that the cancellation has been successfully processed.

## 3. Effect of Cancellation

Cancellation of a subscription generally affects future billing according to the applicable subscription terms.

Depending on the product, customers may retain access until the end of the already-paid billing period.

Cancellation does not automatically guarantee a refund for previous payments.

## 4. Refund Eligibility

A customer may be eligible for a refund when the applicable product or service terms provide for a refund.

Refund eligibility may depend on:

* Date of purchase
* Date of cancellation
* Product type
* Subscription plan
* Usage
* Reason for the request
* Applicable promotional terms
* Applicable agreement

The AI support assistant must not assume that a customer qualifies for a refund.

## 5. Non-Refundable Purchases

Some purchases or services may be non-refundable.

Examples may include certain:

* Custom development services
* Completed services
* Third-party charges
* Promotional purchases
* Usage-based services

The exact refund conditions depend on the applicable product or service terms.

## 6. Refund Request

When a refund request is supported, customers should contact the appropriate support or billing channel.

Customers may be asked to provide:

* Order or invoice number
* Account email address
* Purchase date
* Product or service name
* Reason for the refund request

Customers should never provide passwords, authentication codes, full payment card numbers, or security codes.

## 7. Refund Review

Refund requests may require review by the billing or support team.

The review may determine:

* Whether the purchase is eligible for a refund
* Whether the request meets applicable conditions
* The amount eligible for refund
* The appropriate refund method

The AI support assistant cannot approve a refund unless the connected system explicitly provides that capability.

## 8. Refund Processing

When a refund is approved, the refund is generally processed through the original payment method where supported.

The time required for the refund to appear may depend on:

* Payment provider
* Bank
* Card issuer
* Payment method
* Processing time

NovaTech Solutions cannot guarantee the exact time required for a bank or payment provider to display a completed refund.

## 9. Partial Refunds

Some products or services may qualify for a partial refund rather than a full refund.

Partial refund eligibility depends on the applicable product or service terms.

The AI assistant must not calculate or promise a partial refund unless the applicable policy provides enough information to do so.

## 10. Duplicate Payments

If a customer believes they were charged twice for the same purchase, the issue should be investigated as a possible duplicate payment.

Customers should provide the relevant transaction or invoice information to support.

Duplicate-payment investigations are separate from standard refund requests.

## 11. Failed Transactions

A failed payment does not normally constitute a completed purchase.

Customers should check their account billing history and payment provider statement before submitting a refund request for a failed transaction.

If a payment appears to have been deducted despite a failed transaction, the customer should contact support.

## 12. Accidental Purchases

Customers who believe they made a purchase accidentally should contact support as soon as possible.

Refund eligibility depends on the applicable product and refund terms.

The AI assistant must not guarantee a refund for an accidental purchase.

## 13. Promotional Purchases

Promotional or discounted purchases may have special refund or cancellation conditions.

Customers should review the terms associated with the specific promotion.

The AI assistant must not assume that promotional purchases have the same refund rules as standard purchases.

## 14. Enterprise and Custom Projects

Enterprise contracts and custom development projects may have separate cancellation and refund terms.

These terms are governed by the applicable contract or service agreement.

The AI support assistant must not provide a standard refund decision for an enterprise or custom project without access to the relevant agreement.

## 15. Third-Party Services

If a NovaTech Solutions service involves a third-party provider, charges made directly by that third party may be subject to the third party's refund and cancellation policies.

NovaTech Solutions cannot guarantee refunds for charges controlled by an independent third-party provider.

## 16. Subscription Renewal

Customers should cancel a recurring subscription before the next renewal according to the applicable cancellation process if they do not want the subscription to renew.

A renewal that has already been processed may be subject to the applicable refund policy.

Cancellation and refund eligibility are separate decisions.

## 17. Refund Status

Customers who have already received confirmation that a refund was processed may contact support if the refund has not appeared within the expected payment-provider processing period.

Customers should provide the relevant refund or transaction information when contacting support.

## 18. Policy Exceptions

Any exception to the standard refund policy must be approved by an authorized NovaTech Solutions representative according to company procedures.

The AI support assistant must not promise policy exceptions.

## 19. Support Assistant Rules

When handling refund or cancellation questions, the AI assistant must:

* Explain the documented refund rules.
* Distinguish cancellation from refund.
* Never guarantee a refund without sufficient information.
* Never invent refund deadlines.
* Never invent refund amounts.
* Never promise policy exceptions.
* Never claim that a refund has been approved without confirmation.
* Direct unresolved cases to human billing or support staff.

## 20. Common Refund and Cancellation Questions

### How do I cancel my subscription?

Use the cancellation option available in your account settings or contact customer support if the product does not provide self-service cancellation.

### Does cancelling my subscription automatically give me a refund?

No. Cancellation and refund eligibility are separate. A refund depends on the applicable refund terms.

### Can I get a refund?

Refund eligibility depends on the product, purchase type, subscription terms, and applicable refund policy.

### How long does a refund take?

After a refund is processed, the time required for it to appear depends on the payment provider or bank.

### Can I get a refund for an accidental purchase?

You should contact support as soon as possible. Eligibility depends on the applicable refund terms.

### Are promotional purchases refundable?

Promotional purchases may have specific refund conditions. Check the terms associated with the promotion.

### Can I cancel an enterprise service?

Enterprise cancellation terms are governed by the applicable enterprise agreement or contract.

### My payment failed but money was deducted. What should I do?

Check your billing history and payment-provider statement. If the payment was deducted despite the failed transaction, contact support with the relevant transaction details.

### Can the AI approve my refund?

The AI assistant cannot approve a refund unless the connected system explicitly provides that capability.

### What information should I provide for a refund request?

Provide relevant information such as your account email address, order or invoice number, purchase date, product or service name, and reason for the request. Never provide your password, authentication codes, full card number, or security code.
