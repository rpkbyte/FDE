# NovaTech Solutions — Billing and Payments

## 1. Billing Overview

NovaTech Solutions may charge customers for subscriptions, products, services, usage, or custom projects according to the applicable pricing and service agreement.

Billing details depend on the product or service purchased.

## 2. Payment Methods

Available payment methods depend on the product, customer's location, and billing system.

Supported payment methods may include:

* Credit cards
* Debit cards
* Bank transfers
* Online payment services

The payment methods currently available to a customer are displayed during checkout or communicated in the applicable service agreement.

## 3. Payment Processing

Payments may be processed by NovaTech Solutions or by an authorized third-party payment provider.

Third-party payment providers may have their own:

* Terms of service
* Privacy policies
* Processing fees
* Security requirements
* Payment limitations

NovaTech Solutions does not control the internal systems or policies of third-party payment providers.

## 4. Billing Information

Customers are responsible for providing accurate billing information.

Billing information may include:

* Name
* Billing address
* Email address
* Company information
* Tax information where applicable
* Payment information

Customers should update billing information when it changes.

## 5. Subscription Billing

For recurring subscriptions, customers may be charged according to the billing frequency associated with their subscription.

Possible billing frequencies include:

* Monthly
* Annual
* Custom enterprise billing

The applicable billing schedule is determined by the selected plan or service agreement.

## 6. Billing Date

The billing date depends on when the subscription or service becomes active and the billing schedule associated with the customer's account.

Customers should refer to their account billing information or invoice for the applicable billing date.

## 7. Automatic Payments

Some subscription plans may support automatic recurring payments.

When automatic billing is enabled, the payment method associated with the subscription may be charged automatically according to the applicable billing schedule.

Customers should ensure that their payment method remains valid.

## 8. Failed Payment

A payment may fail for several reasons, including:

* Insufficient funds
* Incorrect payment information
* Expired payment method
* Bank restrictions
* Payment provider restrictions
* Temporary payment processing problems
* Network or technical problems

Customers should verify their payment information and try the payment again when appropriate.

## 9. Failed Recurring Payment

If a recurring subscription payment fails, the customer's account may be subject to restrictions according to the applicable subscription terms.

Possible consequences may include:

* Payment retry
* Temporary restriction of premium features
* Subscription suspension
* Subscription cancellation

The exact process depends on the applicable product and subscription terms.

## 10. Updating a Payment Method

Where supported, customers can update their payment method through their account billing settings.

Customers should ensure that the new payment method is valid before the next billing date.

If a customer cannot update the payment method, they should contact support.

## 11. Invoices

Customers may receive invoices or payment receipts for applicable purchases or subscriptions.

An invoice may contain:

* Customer information
* Invoice number
* Purchase or subscription details
* Billing date
* Amount charged
* Applicable taxes
* Payment status

The exact invoice format depends on the billing system.

## 12. Invoice Problems

If an invoice contains incorrect information, customers should contact customer support or the billing team.

Customers should provide the relevant invoice number when contacting support if available.

## 13. Duplicate Charges

If a customer believes they have been charged more than once for the same transaction, they should:

1. Check their account billing history.
2. Check their payment provider statement.
3. Compare transaction dates and amounts.
4. Contact support if the duplicate charge remains unresolved.

The support team may request transaction information to investigate the issue.

Customers should never send complete payment card numbers, security codes, passwords, or authentication codes.

## 14. Unknown or Unauthorized Charge

If a customer does not recognize a charge, they should first check:

* Their account billing history
* Recent subscriptions
* Recent purchases
* Invoices
* Other authorized users of the account

If the charge remains unrecognized, the customer should contact NovaTech Solutions support.

If the customer believes their payment method itself has been compromised, they should also contact their bank or payment provider.

## 15. Taxes

Applicable taxes may be added to purchases or subscription charges depending on the customer's location and applicable tax rules.

The final amount charged may therefore differ from the base service price.

## 16. Currency

The currency used for billing depends on the product, customer's location, payment system, or applicable agreement.

Customers should review the displayed currency before completing a purchase.

## 17. Refunds and Billing Adjustments

Billing questions and refund requests are handled according to the applicable Refund and Cancellation Policy.

The AI support assistant must not promise a refund or billing adjustment unless the applicable company policy clearly establishes eligibility.

## 18. Cancellation and Future Charges

Customers who cancel a recurring subscription should review the applicable cancellation terms.

Cancellation may prevent future recurring charges, but the exact timing depends on the product and subscription terms.

Customers should verify the cancellation status after completing the cancellation process.

## 19. Enterprise Billing

Enterprise customers may have customized billing arrangements.

These may include:

* Custom invoices
* Purchase orders
* Contract-based billing
* Custom payment schedules
* Bank transfers
* Negotiated payment terms

Enterprise billing is governed by the applicable agreement.

## 20. Payment Security

Customers should never provide sensitive payment credentials to customer support.

The AI support assistant must never request:

* Full credit card numbers
* CVV/security codes
* Bank passwords
* Online banking passwords
* One-time payment authentication codes
* Account passwords
* Private API keys

Customers should use the official payment interface when entering payment information.

## 21. Billing Support Rules

The AI support assistant must:

* Provide only documented billing information.
* Never invent transaction details.
* Never reveal another customer's billing information.
* Never request complete payment credentials.
* Never guarantee a refund without checking the applicable policy.
* Never claim that a payment was successful unless the payment status is available.
* Direct unresolved billing issues to the billing or support team.

## 22. Common Billing Questions

### What payment methods do you accept?

Available payment methods depend on the product and location. Supported options are displayed during checkout or provided in the applicable service agreement.

### Why did my payment fail?

Payments can fail because of insufficient funds, incorrect payment information, an expired payment method, bank restrictions, payment-provider restrictions, or temporary technical problems.

### How can I update my payment method?

If supported, you can update your payment method through your account billing settings.

### Where can I find my invoice?

Invoices or receipts may be available through your account billing section or may be sent to your registered email address, depending on the product.

### I was charged twice. What should I do?

Check your billing history and payment statement first. If the duplicate charge remains unexplained, contact customer support with the relevant transaction information.

### I don't recognize a charge.

Check your billing history, subscriptions, purchases, and invoices. If the charge remains unrecognized, contact support. If you believe your payment method has been compromised, contact your bank or payment provider as well.

### Will I be charged automatically?

Some subscription plans support automatic recurring payments. Check your subscription details to determine whether automatic billing is enabled.

### What happens if my recurring payment fails?

Depending on the applicable subscription terms, the system may retry the payment or temporarily restrict or suspend the subscription.

### Can I get a refund for a payment?

Refund eligibility depends on the company's Refund and Cancellation Policy.

### Should I send my card number to support?

No. Never send complete payment card numbers, CVV/security codes, passwords, or authentication codes to customer support.
