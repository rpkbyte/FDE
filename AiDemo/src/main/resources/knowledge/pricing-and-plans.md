# NovaTech Solutions — Pricing and Plans

## 1. Pricing Overview

NovaTech Solutions offers different pricing structures depending on the product or service being provided.

Pricing may be based on:

* Subscription plans
* One-time purchases
* Custom project pricing
* Usage-based pricing
* Enterprise agreements
* Service contracts

The applicable pricing depends on the specific product or service selected by the customer.

## 2. Standard Subscription Plans

NovaTech Solutions currently offers three standard subscription plans.

### Free Plan

The Free Plan is intended for customers who want to use the basic features of the service without a paid subscription.

Included features may include:

* Basic account access
* Access to selected product features
* Basic customer support
* Limited usage of selected services

The Free Plan does not include features that are specifically restricted to paid plans.

### Professional Plan

The Professional Plan is designed for customers who require additional features and higher usage limits.

The Professional Plan may include:

* All standard Free Plan features
* Additional product functionality
* Higher usage limits
* Additional support capabilities
* Access to selected premium features

The exact features included in the Professional Plan depend on the product.

### Enterprise Plan

The Enterprise Plan is intended for organizations with larger or specialized requirements.

Enterprise customers may receive:

* Custom usage limits
* Custom configurations
* Advanced support
* Dedicated account assistance
* Custom integrations
* Enterprise-level agreements

Enterprise pricing is generally determined according to the customer's requirements and agreed scope.

## 3. Pricing Amounts

The exact monetary price of a product or subscription must be taken from the company's current approved pricing information.

The AI support assistant must not invent or estimate a price.

If the chatbot does not have access to the current price for a specific product or plan, it should tell the customer that the current pricing information is unavailable and direct the customer to the official pricing page or human support.

## 4. Subscription Billing

Paid subscriptions may be billed according to the billing frequency selected by the customer.

Depending on the product, available billing frequencies may include:

* Monthly billing
* Annual billing
* Custom billing for enterprise customers

The customer's applicable billing frequency is determined when the subscription is created or according to the customer's agreement.

## 5. Upgrading a Plan

Customers may be able to upgrade from a lower plan to a higher plan.

An upgrade may provide access to additional features or higher usage limits.

The exact process and pricing adjustment depend on the product and billing system.

Customers who cannot upgrade their plan should contact customer support.

## 6. Downgrading a Plan

Customers may be able to downgrade their subscription depending on the product and applicable subscription terms.

A downgrade may result in:

* Reduced features
* Lower usage limits
* Removal of premium functionality
* Changes to available support

Customers should review the applicable plan information before downgrading.

## 7. Changing Billing Frequency

Where supported, customers may change between available billing frequencies.

For example, a customer may be able to change from monthly billing to annual billing.

Any pricing adjustment or billing change depends on the applicable subscription terms.

## 8. Taxes

Applicable taxes may be added to the displayed or quoted price depending on the customer's location and the applicable tax rules.

The final amount charged to a customer may therefore differ from the base service price.

## 9. Discounts and Promotions

NovaTech Solutions may occasionally offer discounts or promotional pricing.

Promotional offers may have specific:

* Start dates
* End dates
* Eligibility requirements
* Usage restrictions
* Redemption conditions

Promotional pricing should only be provided when the promotion is currently documented as active.

The AI assistant must not invent discount codes or claim that a promotion is active without supporting company information.

## 10. Enterprise Pricing

Enterprise customers may receive customized pricing.

Enterprise pricing can depend on:

* Number of users
* Usage requirements
* Required integrations
* Support requirements
* Infrastructure requirements
* Contract duration
* Custom development requirements

Customers interested in enterprise services should contact the sales or support team.

## 11. Additional Charges

Some products or services may involve additional charges.

Examples may include:

* Third-party service costs
* Additional usage
* Custom development
* Premium support
* Additional storage
* Additional users
* Special integrations

Any additional charges should be communicated to the customer before the charge is applied where applicable.

## 12. Payment Methods

NovaTech Solutions may support multiple payment methods depending on the customer's location and the product.

Supported payment methods may include:

* Credit cards
* Debit cards
* Bank transfers
* Online payment services

The exact payment methods available are shown during checkout or provided in the applicable service agreement.

## 13. Failed Payments

If a payment fails, the customer should verify:

* Payment method details
* Available funds
* Card expiration
* Bank restrictions
* Payment provider status

If the payment continues to fail, the customer should contact support.

A failed payment may result in restricted access or subscription changes according to the applicable billing terms.

## 14. Price Changes

NovaTech Solutions may change its pricing.

Where required, customers will be informed about applicable price changes according to the relevant subscription or service terms.

The AI assistant must not claim that a future price will remain unchanged unless this is explicitly documented.

## 15. Refunds

Pricing and refund information are separate matters.

Customers asking about refunds should be directed to the company's Refund and Cancellation Policy rather than receiving an assumed refund answer.

The AI assistant must not guarantee a refund unless the applicable refund policy clearly establishes the customer's eligibility.

## 16. Customer-Specific Pricing

Some customers may have pricing that differs from publicly available plans.

Customer-specific pricing may result from:

* Enterprise contracts
* Negotiated agreements
* Promotional offers
* Custom services
* Legacy plans

The AI assistant must not disclose or infer another customer's pricing information.

## 17. Pricing Support Rules

When answering pricing questions, the AI support assistant must:

* Use only approved and current pricing information.
* Clearly identify the relevant plan.
* Distinguish monthly and annual pricing when applicable.
* Mention applicable limitations when documented.
* Avoid guessing prices.
* Avoid inventing discounts.
* Avoid promising future prices.
* Avoid revealing confidential customer-specific pricing.

If the required pricing information is unavailable, the assistant should say that it does not have the required information and direct the customer to the official pricing page or human support.
