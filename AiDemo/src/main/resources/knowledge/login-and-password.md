# NovaTech Solutions — Login and Password

## 1. Login Overview

Customers can sign in to their NovaTech Solutions account using the authentication method supported by the relevant product.

A successful login provides access to the features and services associated with the customer's account and subscription.

## 2. Standard Login Process

For products using email and password authentication, customers can generally log in by:

1. Opening the NovaTech Solutions login page.
2. Entering the registered email address.
3. Entering the account password.
4. Selecting the login or sign-in option.

If the credentials are correct, the customer will be signed in.

## 3. Incorrect Password

If the password is incorrect, the customer should verify that:

* Caps Lock is not enabled.
* The correct email address is being used.
* The password is entered correctly.
* There are no unnecessary spaces in the password.

If the password cannot be remembered, the customer should use the password recovery option.

## 4. Forgotten Password

Customers who forget their password should use the password recovery process.

Typical steps are:

1. Open the login page.
2. Select "Forgot Password" or the equivalent password recovery option.
3. Enter the registered email address.
4. Check the email inbox for password recovery instructions.
5. Follow the instructions.
6. Create a new password.

The exact process may vary by product.

## 5. Password Reset Email Not Received

If the password reset email does not arrive, the customer should:

1. Verify that the correct email address was entered.
2. Check the spam or junk folder.
3. Wait a few minutes and try again.
4. Verify that the email account is working correctly.

If the customer still cannot reset the password, they should contact support.

## 6. Account Lockout

Some NovaTech Solutions products may temporarily restrict login attempts after multiple unsuccessful authentication attempts.

This security measure may be used to protect accounts against unauthorized access.

Customers experiencing an account lockout should follow the recovery instructions provided by the product or contact customer support.

The AI assistant must not provide methods for bypassing security controls.

## 7. Password Requirements

Password requirements may differ between NovaTech Solutions products.

Where supported, customers should use a password that:

* Is sufficiently long.
* Is difficult to guess.
* Does not contain easily predictable personal information.
* Is not reused across important accounts.

Customers should follow the password requirements displayed during account registration or password creation.

## 8. Changing a Password

Customers who know their current password may be able to change it through account settings.

A typical process is:

1. Sign in to the account.
2. Open account or security settings.
3. Select the password change option.
4. Enter the required information.
5. Create and confirm the new password.
6. Save the change.

The exact process depends on the product.

## 9. Suspected Account Compromise

If a customer believes someone has accessed their account without permission, they should:

1. Change the account password immediately if possible.
2. Review recent account activity if the product provides this feature.
3. Sign out of unfamiliar sessions if supported.
4. Contact NovaTech Solutions support.

Customers should also secure the email account associated with their NovaTech Solutions account.

## 10. Login From a New Device

Customers may be able to sign in from multiple supported devices.

Some products may require additional verification when a customer signs in from a new or unfamiliar device.

The required verification method depends on the product's security configuration.

## 11. Login Problems

If a customer cannot log in, they should check:

* Internet connectivity.
* Email address.
* Password.
* Account status.
* Browser or application version.
* Service availability.

Customers should also check whether the service is experiencing a known outage.

If the issue continues, they should contact customer support.

## 12. Browser Troubleshooting

For browser-based products, customers experiencing login problems can try:

* Refreshing the page.
* Closing and reopening the browser.
* Clearing cached website data.
* Trying a supported browser.
* Temporarily disabling problematic browser extensions.
* Trying a private or incognito browser window.

Customers should avoid sharing passwords or authentication codes while requesting technical support.

## 13. Mobile Application Login Problems

For mobile applications, customers can try:

* Checking their internet connection.
* Restarting the application.
* Updating the application.
* Restarting the device.
* Checking whether the service is available.
* Reinstalling the application if appropriate.

If the issue continues, the customer should contact support.

## 14. Authentication Codes

Some NovaTech Solutions products may use additional authentication methods such as one-time verification codes.

Customers must never share authentication codes with another person.

NovaTech Solutions support representatives should not request a customer's authentication code.

## 15. Social or Third-Party Login

Some products may support authentication through third-party identity providers.

The exact available providers depend on the product.

If third-party authentication fails, customers should verify that:

* They are using the correct third-party account.
* The third-party service is available.
* The required permissions were granted.
* The NovaTech Solutions account is correctly associated with the third-party identity.

## 16. Support Security Rules

The AI support assistant must never request or store:

* Account passwords
* One-time passwords
* Authentication codes
* API secret keys
* Private encryption keys
* Recovery codes

The assistant should never instruct customers to bypass authentication or security mechanisms.

## 17. Common Login Questions

### I cannot log in. What should I do?

Verify your email address and password, check your internet connection, and make sure the service is available. If you forgot your password, use the password recovery option.

### I forgot my password.

Use the "Forgot Password" option on the login page and follow the password recovery instructions.

### I did not receive the password reset email.

Check your spam or junk folder, verify that you entered the correct email address, and try the recovery process again.

### My account is locked.

Some products temporarily restrict login attempts after repeated failed authentication attempts. Follow the recovery instructions provided by the product or contact support.

### Can I change my password?

If the product supports password changes, you can normally change your password through your account or security settings.

### Someone may have accessed my account. What should I do?

Change your password immediately if possible and contact NovaTech Solutions support to report the suspected unauthorized access.

### Can I tell support my password?

No. Never share your password with support or anyone else.

### Can I share my authentication code with support?

No. Authentication codes and one-time passwords should never be shared with support representatives or other people.

### Why can't I log in from my new device?

Some products may require additional verification for new or unfamiliar devices. Complete the verification process provided by the product.

### The mobile app is not letting me log in.

Check your internet connection, restart the application, make sure the application is updated, and try again. If the problem continues, contact support.

### The website login page is not working.

Check your internet connection, refresh the page, try a supported browser, and check whether the service is experiencing an outage. Contact support if the problem continues.
