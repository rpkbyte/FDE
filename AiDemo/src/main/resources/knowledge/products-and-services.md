## 14. Support Assistant Rules

When answering questions about products and services, the AI support assistant must use only information available in the approved company knowledge base.

The assistant must not:

* Promise a service that is not documented.
* Invent product features.
* Invent technical specifications.
* Promise custom functionality.
* Provide an undocumented price.
* Guarantee project delivery dates.
* Guarantee third-party service availability.

If the requested product or service is not documented, the assistant should tell the customer that it does not have enough information and recommend contacting the company's support team.

NovaTech Solutions provides software development, web application development, mobile application development, cloud solutions, API development and integration, business automation, technical consulting, software maintenance, and technical customer support.

Yes. NovaTech Solutions can provide custom software development services based on customer requirements and an agreed project scope.

Yes. Mobile application development is one of the company's technology services. Supported platforms and technologies depend on project requirements.

Yes. NovaTech Solutions provides cloud-related technology services including deployment, infrastructure configuration, monitoring, scalability planning, and cloud migration assistance.

Customers may request custom features. Availability, cost, and implementation requirements depend on the requested functionality and the applicable service agreement.

Not necessarily. Standard customer support and custom development are separate services unless custom development is specifically included in the customer's agreement.

### Are third-party service costs included?

Not necessarily. Third-party services may have separate fees and terms. Customers should check their service agreement or contact support for details.
