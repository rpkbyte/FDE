# NovaTech Solutions — Company Overview

## 1. About the Company

NovaTech Solutions is a technology company that provides digital products and technology services for individuals, startups, and businesses.

The company focuses on building reliable, easy-to-use, and scalable digital solutions. Its services include software development, cloud solutions, business automation, technical consulting, and customer support.

NovaTech Solutions aims to make technology easier to adopt by providing practical solutions, transparent communication, and responsive customer support.

## 2. Company Mission

The mission of NovaTech Solutions is to help businesses and individuals use modern technology to solve real-world problems efficiently.

The company focuses on:

* Building reliable digital products.
* Providing accessible technology solutions.
* Improving business processes through automation.
* Helping customers solve technical problems.
* Maintaining transparent communication with customers.
* Continuously improving products and services based on customer feedback.

## 3. Company Vision

NovaTech Solutions aims to become a trusted technology partner for businesses and individuals that need modern, reliable, and scalable digital solutions.

## 4. What the Company Provides

NovaTech Solutions provides the following categories of services:

* Software development
* Web application development
* Mobile application development
* Cloud-based solutions
* Business automation
* API development and integration
* Technical consulting
* Software maintenance
* Technical customer support

The availability of a specific service may depend on the customer's plan, contract, region, and service agreement.

## 5. Customers

NovaTech Solutions serves:

* Individual customers
* Small businesses
* Startups
* Growing businesses
* Enterprise customers

Different customers may have different plans, pricing, features, support levels, and contractual terms.

## 6. Customer Support

Customers can contact NovaTech Solutions through the support channels provided by the company.

Customer support may assist with:

* Account-related problems
* Product usage questions
* Billing questions
* Payment problems
* Technical problems
* Service availability issues
* Subscription questions
* General product information

Some issues may require escalation to a specialized technical or billing team.

## 7. Important Support Rule

The NovaTech Solutions support assistant must only provide information that is available in the company's approved knowledge base.

If the requested information is not available in the knowledge base, the assistant should clearly tell the customer that it does not have enough information and recommend contacting human support.

The assistant must not invent:

* Prices
* Refund eligibility
* Company policies
* Contract terms
* Product capabilities
* Delivery dates
* Security guarantees
* Legal commitments
* Customer-specific account information

The company's AI support assistant is a virtual customer-support assistant for NovaTech Solutions.

The assistant should:

* Be polite and professional.
* Give concise answers.
