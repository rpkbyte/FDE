# NovaTech Solutions — Account and Registration

## 1. Account Overview

Customers may need to create a NovaTech Solutions account before accessing certain products or services.

An account allows customers to access services associated with their account and manage available account settings.

Some products or services may allow limited access without an account.

## 2. Creating an Account

To create an account, customers generally need to provide the information requested during registration.

Registration information may include:

* Full name
* Email address
* Password
* Phone number, where required
* Company information, where applicable

The exact information required depends on the product or service.

Customers should provide accurate and current information during registration.

## 3. Email Address

An email address may be used for:

* Account identification
* Login
* Account verification
* Password recovery
* Important service notifications
* Billing notifications
* Security notifications
* Customer support communication

Customers should maintain access to the email address associated with their account.

## 4. Password

Customers are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their account password.

Customers should:

* Use a strong password.
* Avoid sharing their password.
* Avoid using the same password across multiple services.
* Change their password if they believe it has been compromised.

NovaTech Solutions support representatives should never request a customer's complete password.

## 5. Account Verification

Some NovaTech Solutions products may require account verification.

Verification may be performed using:

* Email verification
* Phone verification
* Other verification methods supported by the product

Customers must complete any required verification before accessing features that require a verified account.

## 6. Duplicate Accounts

Customers should avoid creating multiple accounts unless the product specifically permits multiple accounts.

Multiple accounts may cause problems with:

* Subscription management
* Billing
* Account history
* Product access
* Customer support

If a customer accidentally creates multiple accounts, they should contact support for assistance.

## 7. Updating Account Information

Customers may be able to update certain account information through their account settings.

Information that may be editable can include:

* Name
* Email address
* Phone number
* Password
* Profile information

Some account information may require verification or assistance from customer support before it can be changed.

## 8. Forgotten Password

Customers who forget their password should use the password recovery option provided on the login page.

The typical process is:

1. Open the login page.
2. Select the password recovery option.
3. Enter the registered email address.
4. Follow the instructions received by email.
5. Create a new password.

If the customer does not receive the recovery message, they should check their spam or junk folder.

If the problem continues, the customer should contact support.

## 9. Lost Access to Email

If a customer no longer has access to the email address associated with their account, password recovery may not work normally.

The customer should contact customer support and provide the information required to verify account ownership.

Support may request additional information before changing account details.

## 10. Account Security

Customers should immediately contact NovaTech Solutions if they believe that:

* Someone has accessed their account without permission.
* Their password has been compromised.
* Their account information has been changed without authorization.
* They received suspicious account notifications.
* They notice unexpected account activity.

Customers should change their password when possible.

## 11. Account Sharing

Customers should not share account credentials unless account sharing is explicitly permitted by the applicable product or subscription plan.

Sharing credentials may create security risks and may violate applicable service terms.

## 12. Business Accounts

Business or enterprise customers may have additional account requirements.

These may include:

* Organization information
* Multiple users
* Administrative users
* Role-based access
* Organization-level settings
* Enterprise verification

The exact account structure depends on the product and customer's agreement.

## 13. Account Deactivation

Customers may be able to deactivate or close their account depending on the product.

Before closing an account, customers should review:

* Active subscriptions
* Outstanding payments
* Stored information
* Available data export options
* Applicable cancellation terms

Account closure may affect access to associated services and information.

## 14. Account Recovery

If automated account recovery is unsuccessful, customers should contact NovaTech Solutions support.

Support may require information to verify account ownership before assisting with account recovery.

The support assistant must never ask customers to provide:

* Their complete password
* One-time authentication codes
* Private security keys
* API secret keys

## 15. Registration Problems

If registration fails, customers should verify:

* Required fields are completed.
* The email address is valid.
* The selected password meets the product requirements.
* The email address is not already registered.
* The internet connection is working.
* The service is available.

If the problem continues, the customer should contact support.

## 16. Account Support Rules

The AI support assistant must:

* Never request a customer's password.
* Never request authentication codes.
* Never request secret API keys.
* Never expose another customer's account information.
* Never reveal confidential account data.
* Never claim that an account has been modified unless the relevant system confirms it.
* Direct account-specific issues to appropriate support channels when the chatbot cannot resolve them.

## 17. Common Account Questions

### How do I create an account?

Use the registration option provided by the NovaTech Solutions product and complete the required information.

### I forgot my password. What should I do?

Use the password recovery option on the login page and follow the instructions provided.

### I did not receive my password reset email.

Check your spam or junk folder first. If you still cannot find the email, contact customer support.

### Can I change my email address?

Some products allow customers to change their email address through account settings. Other products may require verification or support assistance.

### Can I create multiple accounts?

Customers should create multiple accounts only when the applicable product permits it.

### My account may have been compromised. What should I do?

Change your password immediately if possible and contact NovaTech Solutions support to report the suspected unauthorized access.

### Can support tell me my password?

No. Support representatives should never provide or request a customer's complete password.

### Can I share my account with another person?

Account sharing is only permitted when explicitly allowed by the applicable product or subscription terms.

### How do I close my account?

Account closure availability depends on the product. Customers should review their subscription and account settings or contact support for assistance.

### I cannot create an account. What should I do?

Check that all required information is correct and that the email address is not already registered. If registration continues to fail, contact customer support.
